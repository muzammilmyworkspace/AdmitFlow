## 4. Academic entry rules that matter for our students

- **Minimum grades:** VILNIUS TECH and KTU and VMU: CGPA ≥ 60% (VMU: 55% Maghreb, 65% Egypt). **VU for Pakistan: ≥ 75% total marks** (bachelor); master CGPA 3.00 / 75%.
- **OMPT mathematics exam:** VILNIUS TECH requires it (≥60%) for Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal; VU requires it (≥55%) for some master programmes for Pakistani applicants.
- **Study gap:** VILNIUS TECH rejects a gap of more than three years since the last completed education.
- **LSMU:** entrance test (interview + 30 Biology + 30 Chemistry MCQs, 90 min, online); no scholarships for English-taught study; deposit of half a year's tuition (Medicine/Dentistry/Pharmacy/Vet) or a full year (others).
- **ISM:** state mathematics exam or ISM math test; same tuition for EU and non-EU at bachelor level.
- **LCC:** 40% grades / 30% English / 30% interview; generous need-based aid.

## 5. Documents — the checklist the product should show

Application stage (per university, all need legalised + translated copies):
1. Passport (valid ≥ 1–2 years)
2. Secondary certificate + transcripts (bachelor) / degree + supplement (master)
3. English certificate (≤ 2 years old at LCC)
4. Motivation letter (VU 1200–4000 chars; VMU 1200–3000)
5. CV in English (masters; VMU, ISM)
6. Photo 3×4 cm
7. Application fee payment (€100–180, non-refundable; Flywire at VU/VMU)
8. SKVC recognition where the university does not run it (VU, VILNIUS TECH)

Legalisation: apostille (Hague) or **double legalisation** (non-Hague — Pakistan): issuing-country MFA → Lithuanian MFA. VU additionally wants **HEC + MOFA attestation** for Pakistani certificates.

Residence-permit stage (after the offer):
9. Mediation letter from the university (use within 7–14 days)
10. Bank statement — 2026: €577/month × 12 + €1,153 (VU) ≈ €8,077; if a parent's account, doubled + notarised commitment (VMU guide)
11. Health insurance ≥ €10,000, Schengen-wide, no franchise, ≥ 1 year
12. Police clearance certificate (every country lived in > 6 months in last 2 years; legalised; valid 6 months)
13. Accommodation confirmation (dormitory letter ≈ €50 at VMU)
14. State fee €80 (2 months) / €320 (1 month) + VFS charge €40–160

## 6. Migration — open risk for Pakistan

- Degree students need a **Temporary Residence Permit**; Study in Lithuania says apply for national visa D **and** TRP before travelling. Universities issue a mediation letter; the student files in MIGRIS and gives biometrics at a **VFS Global** centre.
- VFS accepts Lithuanian TRP applications in ~30 countries (Turkey, Azerbaijan, India, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan… — full list in the JSON). **Pakistan is not one of them**, Lithuania has no embassy in Pakistan, and the Embassy of Hungary in Islamabad handles only short-stay C visas for Lithuania. LSMU states this explicitly. Several agency sites claim students enter on a D visa and file the TRP after arrival, but that contradicts the official "apply before you come" guidance.
- **Before SNZ takes a Pakistani student's €20, the exact route must be confirmed in writing with the Migration Department (migracija.lt) and the admitting university.** This is the single biggest operational risk in the Lithuania launch and belongs in docs/55 as a BLOCKING item.
- Financial proof, insurance and PCC rules are in the JSON (`crossCutting.migration`).

## 8. How this loads into the product

- `Country` LT with `status = LIVE`; `University.type = UNIVERSITY`; `UniversityMetadata.source` = "official website", `sourceUrl`, `verifiedAt = 2026-09-20`, `confidence` from the JSON.
- `Program` rows only where a programme name and level are known; `TuitionFee.amount` null-safe (omit the row rather than invent a number).
- `EnglishRequirement` rows per test where a minimum is published; `ProgramRequirement` MIN_GPA rows for the 60% / 75% rules with the country condition in `description`.
- `DocumentRequirement` from §5 items 1–8 as programme-level mandatory documents; items 9–14 belong to a post-offer "residence permit" checklist.
- Stale rule already in the codebase: anything with `verifiedAt` older than the threshold shows the stale label (docs/26).
